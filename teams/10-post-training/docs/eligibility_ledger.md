Generated by `sft/mix_inventory.py` from the documents on this branch. Every number below is read from a record or computed from one. Rebuild with `python sft/mix_inventory.py build --ocr2-runs <folder> --verify`; the folder (`report_0-69.json` and its 70 shard records) is a private run folder that is not on the branch, so without it the OCR-2 row-level lines (length percentiles, per-source tokens, context fit) are not recomputed. ## 6. Decisions for Sai and TSAI

Each item is something the records cannot settle. The final shares and the budget unit are theirs.

1. **Budget unit.** The proposal says "token/trajectory". This ledger uses total processed non-padding tokens (`tokens`) for the budget and reports supervised targets beside it. For OCR-2 the targets are 93.5% of tokens; for an agent set with long tool results the share is lower (the 1,000-row test in `convert_report.md` shows 28.3% for SWE-rebench, 93.2% for codeforces-cots, prefix samples only). Choose the unit; a supervised-token budget and a total-token budget give different supply.
2. **Safety share.** The active table in `sft_mix_proposal.md` section 2 says 7 General / 3 Safety, while sections 6 and 8 say to use 0 Safety / 10 General until TSAI names an allowed safety set with chat rows. All five safety sets are excluded, or text-only with no assistant turn (Aegis). Scenario `proposal_table` therefore requests 3% from sources that cannot supply it (INFEASIBLE above). Pick `plan_d3` (0 / 10) or name an allowed set. The table still names four excluded safety sets (section 5).
3. **Model context length.** Not set, and it is a blocker named by the OCR-2 pack check (`context_limit_not_set`). OCR-2 kept rows that fit whole: 414,211 of 427,152 at 16,384 tokens and all at 32,768 (section 3).
4. **Token counts for the other scanned sets.** 32 scanned sets have row counts but no token record. Their supply, the shifted-target share, length percentiles and effort mix cannot be filled by estimation. The next step is one tokenizing pass over each set's clean file (`convert.py --tokenizer`, then `--token-records`). The clean files are local outputs of the decontamination runs and are not on the branch. The frozen tokenizer arrives on 14 Nov, so a pass now would need a rerun.
5. **OCR-2 admission.** 0 rows are admitted. Blockers in the pack report (union over the 70 shards): `context_limit_not_set`, `join_not_in_admission_mode`, `set_not_allowed_in_licence_table`, `set_not_allowed_in_policy`, `source_identity_not_verified:apps/train`, `source_identity_not_verified:code_contests/train`, `source_identity_not_verified:taco/train`, `source_not_approved_for_training:apps`, `source_not_approved_for_training:code_contests`, `source_not_approved_for_training:taco`, `tokenizer_not_approved`. 69,690 `open-r1/codeforces` train rows are held until the producer revision is established.
6. **Overlap between sets.** Family hashes exist only for OCR-2, so duplication of problems between `sft-code-opencodereasoning`, `sft-code-opencodereasoning-2` and `sft-code-codeforces-cots` is not measured. Do not sum their families as if they were disjoint until it is.
7. **Indic.** Odia has no rows in `aya_dataset` and Kannada 334; the Indic area has no token record at all. The proposal asks for a per-subset cap on `aya_collection` ("a few thousand rows") without a number, so no capped scenario is computed. Section 3 of the proposal gives a 10 / 5 split between `aya_dataset` and `aya_collection`; section 7 proposes a different composition without shares. Say which.
8. **Partial and held coverage.** `sft-math-nemotron-proofs-v3-sft` converted 293,648 of 414,890 source rows. 5 sets in the licence table are not excluded yet have no converter or decontam record (the held list in section 2). Run them or drop them.
9. **What to call what.** 34 scanned entries, 0 approved complete training packs. Diagnostic scan, eligible retained subset, approved pack and trained result are four milestones; only the first exists.
