# Tokens per word with the frozen tokenizer p1536 `e6b93a93…`

Made by `harness/fertility_domains.py`. Outside tokenizer with a bigger vocabulary: o200k_base (200,019 ids).

- **Word rule (all languages):** maximal runs of non-whitespace (str.split()). tokens/word = tokens / words of the same text; no bos or eos.
- **Encoder speed, one core:** p1536 392,958 tokens/s, o200k 1,716,336 tokens/s (`tokenizers` 0.23.3, macOS-26.3-arm64-arm-64bit).
- **Lossless:** decode(encode(x)) == normalizer(x) for every document in every table below.
