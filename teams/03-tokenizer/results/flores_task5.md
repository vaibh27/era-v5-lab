# FLORES-200 devtest: p1536 `e6b93a93…` vs published tokenizers (Task 5)

Made by `harness/flores_task5.py`. FLORES is used to report on the frozen tokenizer, not to choose one.

- **Word rule (all languages):** maximal runs of non-whitespace (str.split()); no word segmentation for Zh, Ja.
- **Encoding:** each of the 1,012 devtest sentences per language is encoded alone, with `add_special_tokens=False`.
- **Mean:** the 11 Indic languages (Hi … Or). Sanskrit and the reference languages are not in it.

## Where p1536 loses

A lossless tokenizer here (at least 98% of sentences round trip) needs fewer tokens for the same text.
- **WordPiece encoders are left out.** IndicBERTv2 and MuRIL lose text (0–75% of sentences round trip): they split on whitespace and punctuation first and map unknown words to [UNK]. Their counts are not comparable with a byte-level BPE, and they are encoder vocabularies, not ones for a generative model.
- **Krutrim-2 and Sarvam-M give the same counts everywhere.** Both use Mistral's Tekken vocabulary (131,072 ids); the merges are identical and only 10 control-token names differ ([IMG], <pad>, [PREFIX], …).
